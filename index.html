Where should Tom live next?

A plain-English summary of the US relocation search so far: what Tom is looking for, which places made it, which didn't, and why.

Starting point: Coral Springs, FL Report date: 10 Oct 2026 Latest scout: 30 Sep 2026

The short version

Four things to know right now.

Best new option
East Forsyth County, GA

Cumming / Pilgrim Mill side of Lake Lanier. The only new place to pass every screen in the latest scout, including 3 Turkish and 2 Sichuan spots within ~30 min.

Parked at Tom's request
Happy Valley / W. Damascus, OR

The cleanest full-stack hit of the US search and the head-to-head winner over Duvall, but Tom isn't sure about Oregon.

Stretch-budget option
Duvall / Snoqualmie Valley, WA

Better Cascades and food, no state income tax, but acreage runs ~$980K+ and there is flood risk.

Key insight
Food is the bottleneck

The hardest part is finding 2+ real Sichuan and 2+ real Turkish restaurants within ~40 min of affordable mountain-and-lake acreage.

Tom's checklist

Every place was judged against these. The highlighted one is what knocked out the most places.

  • Four true seasonsReal winters, not just a cool spell.
  • Low crimeSafe day-to-day living.
  • A couple of acres (~1–3)At roughly Coral Springs house money (~$680–720K). Current lot is ~0.22 acre.
  • Mountains and lakes nearbyBoth within about 45–60 minutes.
  • Little dangerous wildlifeIn daily life, not just on hikes.
  • Good schoolsGood colleges plus solid K-12 / charter options.
  • Museums within ~60 minCulture within an easy day trip.
  • Good infrastructureAirport, hospital, broadband.
  • Multiple real Sichuan AND multiple real Turkish restaurantsWithin a ~35–40 min weeknight drive. Real Sichuan = mapo, dan dan, mala, hot pot. Real Turkish = pide, lahmacun, kebab, Iskender. Gyro-only or daytime-only cafes don't count.
  • Stay in the USCanada was explored, then dropped.
